Thirty-two teeth, numbered the way dentists number them (FDI system). Tap any tooth to mark pain, sensitivity, a filling — or that it is perfectly fine. Your notes stay on this device only.

How to read it: the top row is your upper teeth, the bottom row your lower teeth — shown as if you are facing a mirror. Numbers 11–18 are the upper right, 21–28 the upper left, 31–38 the lower left, 41–48 the lower right.
